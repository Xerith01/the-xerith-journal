## Start with curiosity

Not every useful idea arrives fully formed. Sometimes it begins as a question, a small experiment, or a note about something that did not make sense yesterday.

This journal is a place to keep those moments. Expect thoughtful notes on learning, technology, cybersecurity, and the process of turning information into understanding.

### The principle

**Stay curious. Build carefully. Write it down.**

This is the beginning, not the finished story.
